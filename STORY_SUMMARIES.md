## Story 1. 「수칙 4번은 거짓말입니다」 (Rule No. 4 Is a Lie)

**The world:** Ghosts come to a district office at night (10 PM to 6 AM) to file complaints.
A list of 7 rules on the desk is supposed to keep the night worker alive.

**Main rules of this world:**
- If the night worker dies before 6 AM, the night starts again at 10 PM.
- Every restart takes away one memory.
- Saying something false against the official records makes you disappear.
- The desk does not go back in time. Things on it stay and get old.

**People:**
- **Kang Seo-ha (강서하):** new junior civil servant, first day at work. She can't say "no".
- **No Mi-ran (노미란):** her team leader. Pushes her own night duty onto Seo-ha.
- **The Senior (선임):** an old night worker. Reads the rules aloud.
- **Ji-ho (지호):** Seo-ha's little brother. Only heard on the phone.
- **Nam Jae-woo (남재우):** the worker before Seo-ha (from EP02).

### EP01 「수칙 4번」 (Rule 4)

**Night 1 (she dies):**
- The team leader gives Seo-ha the night duty but keeps her own name on the duty list.
- A ghost asks, "Who is in charge?"
- Rule 4 says: answer "I am in charge." Seo-ha says it.
- That is false (the team leader is in charge). She dies.

**The night restarts:**
- She is back at 10 PM, alive.
- But she forgot the promise she made to her little brother.

**Night 2 (she wins):**
- She knows what will happen.
- She refuses Rule 4 and tells the truth: "My team leader is in charge."
- The ghost goes to the team leader.
- The team leader must write down that she pushed her work onto Seo-ha.

**Big twist:** The rule sheet has the same sticker as Seo-ha's notebook, but old and faded.
Seo-ha wrote these rules herself in earlier loops and forgot. Only Rule 4 was added by someone else.

**Ending:** A hand with red tape appears behind her: "You still haven't changed that rule?"

**Goal of EP01:** Make readers ask, "Who added the deadly Rule 4?"

### EP02 「퇴근하세요」 (Go Home)

**What happens:**
- The hand belongs to a real person: Jae-woo, the worker before her who "quit after 3 days".
- He knows her from earlier loops: "You followed Rule 4 again, didn't you?"
- He offers to cover Rule 4 with red tape. Seo-ha says "no" to him.
  She wants to find out who added it. Only that person can remove it.
- At 5:59 AM a voice says, "You can go home now."
  It is Jae-woo holding a handover form. "Go home" means "I will stay here instead of you."
- Seo-ha says "no" again, so Jae-woo won't be stuck in her place. That was her only way out.
- 6 AM: the night is over and becomes final.
- In the morning the team leader tries to tear out her confession, but Seo-ha stops her with the rules.
- Her brother calls. She can't remember the promise, so she lies: "Of course I'll come."

**Ending:** 9 AM. The team leader pushes the same handover form at Seo-ha: "Just sign here. Right?"

**Goal of EP02:** Show who the hand belongs to, and make readers ask, "Will she sign?"

### EP03 「인수인계」 (Handover)

**What happens:**
- Seo-ha puts the pen down and reads the whole form, even the back.
  The last line says she also becomes "the person in charge" on the duty list.
- Her tricks stop working. The bosses take the team leader's side.
  "If you don't sign, a new worker comes next month. They won't read the back."
- To protect the next new worker, Seo-ha signs, knowing the danger. Her first loss.
- Night 2: a ghost asks, "Who is in charge?" She says, "I am in charge."
  The same words killed her before. Now they are true, so she lives.
- A new ghost says, "I want to move into this room." She almost says "no",
  but saying "no" would let it come inside. She accepts the form, then rejects it the proper way.

**Ending:** 3 AM. A name-change form comes in: "The new name I want is… Kang Seo-ha."

**Goal of EP03:** End of the free episodes. Make readers ask, "Who wants to take her name, and what happens to her?"

## Story 2. 「독은 제가 먼저 먹겠습니다」 (I'll Taste the Poison First)

**The world:** A royal palace. Food tasters eat the royal food first to check for poison.
A silver needle is used to test food. Palace law: a taster who misses poison dies,
and a taster who reports poison that isn't there also dies.

**People:**
- **Chae-ryeong (채령), 17:** a food taster. She lives under her dead older sister's name, **Dan-i (단이)**.
  Her family sold medicine herbs, so she knows poisons. That is her secret.
- **The prince (왕자), 8:** trusts only her.
- **Court Lady Min (민 상궁):** head of the tasters. Always kind and smiling.
- **Inspector Baek (백 감찰):** strict palace inspector. Suspects Chae-ryeong.
- **Cook Hong (홍 숙수):** the palace cook.
- **Physician Won (원 의관):** a gentle royal doctor (from EP03).

### EP01 「독은 제가 먼저 먹겠습니다」 (I'll Taste the Poison First)

**What happens:**
- Chae-ryeong tastes the prince's lunch. The silver needle stays clean.
- But she notices poison on the **rim** of the bowl, which the needle can't catch.
- If she speaks, people learn her secret. If she stays quiet, the prince dies.
- She grabs the prince's wrist and proves it with vinegar and ash: the rim turns light purple.
  "Silver can lie. My tongue can't." The prince is saved.
- The inspector asks, "Where did a taster learn a medicine seller's trick?"
- Cook Hong is blamed and taken away.

**Big twist:** Court Lady Min's handkerchief has the same purple stain.
The poison was not for the prince. It was **bait to find the girl** who can spot it.
The kind court lady set the trap.

**Ending:** Court Lady Min says her real name: "Chae-ryeong. Taste again tomorrow. This time, keep quiet."
Chae-ryeong does not answer.

**Goal of EP01:** Show her skill and her secret, then make readers ask, "What does Court Lady Min want from her?"

### EP02 「은침이 검게 변한 아침」 (The Morning the Needle Turned Black)

**What happens:**
- Chae-ryeong sits down to taste again (her answer is to obey).
- The silver needle turns dark on the prince's egg porridge. If it is poison, the kitchen staff will be punished.
- Small twist: the prince asked for this porridge himself. His mother used to make it for him.
- Chae-ryeong doesn't reveal what she knows. She eats the whole bowl and waits:
  "If it is poison, I die first. That is my job." She is fine. The kitchen is saved.
- The inspector suspects her more: "The needle was black and you weren't scared. You knew."

**Big twist:** At night, Court Lady Min shows an antidote pill hidden in her sleeve.
"Yesterday's amount would not kill anyone. If you hadn't stopped it, I would have knocked the bowl over myself."
She was never trying to kill the prince. Why she set the trap: "Not yet."

**Ending:** "Tomorrow the royal clinic sends medicine. Whatever is in it, just say it is clean."
Chae-ryeong does not answer.

**Goal of EP02:** Court Lady Min is not the prince's killer, but her orders get heavier: from "keep quiet" to "lie".

### EP03 「깨끗합니다」 (It's Clean)

**What happens:**
- Kind Physician Won brings the prince's medicine himself.
- Chae-ryeong tastes it and her tongue tingles: something is inside.
- Small twist: the medicine came sealed straight from the clinic. Court Lady Min never touched it.
  So **someone else** is poisoning the prince.
- She says "It's clean," as ordered. Then she plays a tiger game with the prince so he spills the medicine.
  He never drinks it.
- The cloth used to wipe it turns light purple after it is washed.

**Big twist:** Court Lady Min knew something was in the medicine.
She made Chae-ryeong say "clean" so the poisoner would not know anyone noticed.
"I am not the one who puts poison. I am hunting the one who does. A hunt needs a good nose."

**Ending:** Inspector Baek holds up the purple cloth: "You said it was clean. That was a lie. Show me your palace entry tag."
The tag belongs to her dead sister. Chae-ryeong does not answer.

**Goal of EP03:** Show a new hidden enemy, and make readers ask, "Will the inspector find out who she really is?"

## Story 3. 「맑은 날의 우산」 (Umbrella on a Sunny Day)

**The world:** A market entrance in the rainy season. A romantic comedy with one small piece of magic.

**People:**
- **So Dan-bi (소단비):** runs a shaved-ice stall. When her feelings get strong,
  a small cloud rains **only on her**. She hides it with an umbrella.
- **Mun Tae-o (문태오):** an umbrella repairman. Quiet. He sees her rain but never says so.
- **Go Ok-ja (고옥자):** head of the market.
- **Gong Man-sik (공만식):** fish-cake seller across the way.
- **Jin Bo-mi (진보미):** a little girl, a regular customer.

### EP01 「맑은 날의 비」 (Rain on a Sunny Day)

**What happens:**
- Sunny day, but rain falls only on Dan-bi. Wind flips her pink umbrella and breaks one rib.
- A yellow umbrella, **already open**, comes over her head. It is Tae-o.
  He looks at her, not up at the cloud.
- They both have the same key to the same small shop space.
  The market head made a mistake and gave it to both of them.
  Ruling: share it half and half until the rainy season ends. A tape line goes down the middle.
- They argue all day. Dan-bi hides her broken umbrella.
- A child almost notices the cloud above Dan-bi. Tae-o calls the child away. Dan-bi is relieved.
- At night, without a word, Tae-o fixes her umbrella with a new rib and yellow thread.
- Next morning her umbrella blocks her rain perfectly for the first time. She is so flustered
  that her rain gets louder and covers the one thing Tae-o says to her.

**Big twist:** The sun comes out and Tae-o looks up at her cloud for the first time.
He saw her secret rain from the very first moment and kept it to himself.

**Ending:** One raindrop on her umbrella shines like gold.

**Goal of EP01:** A sweet first meeting, then make readers ask,
"How long has he known, how much does he know, and what did he say?"
