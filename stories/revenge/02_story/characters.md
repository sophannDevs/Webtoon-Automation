## 2. Keo Visal — the Hero, "the Cameraman" (អ្នកថត)

### 2-1. Basics
- 17, Grade 11, class 11C. Last row, window side, corner single desk (world §2-2). Quietly good grades (rank around 6th,
  never named, because he cannot pay for extra class).
- Lives with his mother in a small rented room (LOC_HERO_ROOM). Father died three years ago of an illness (off-page,
  never shown). Rides an old bicycle.
- **The job:** since Grade 10 semester 2 he films the circle's "jokes" on Dara's phone at midday and deletes them in front
  of Rachana at the 13:55 bell. He must say "Rolling." (ថត។) at the start of every clip (world R3).

### 2-2. Want / Need / Fear
- **Want:** to stop being the Cameraman, and for the four of them to lose exactly what they made him lose: face, in
  front of the people who matter. Proportional, in the room, never posted (E1).
- **Need:** to use **his own voice**, not only borrowed recordings, and to forgive (and confess) his own silence. The
  arc ends when he says the true thing about himself out loud, not when the last clip plays.
- **Fear:** the phone being found or confiscated (R4); his mother learning what he did for a year and a half; that he is
  "the same as them" because his voice is on every clip.

### 2-3. Flaw (fatal)
- **He stopped expecting help, so he stopped asking.** He treats people as audiences and voices, not as people: cold,
  exact, patient to the point of using others' silence as a tool. This makes him miss allies (Kolab, Sophy, Mealea) and
  risk the cost beats (T-H3): he will be tempted to play a clip that also shames a bystander.

### 2-4. Secrets (layered)
1. **The archive:** the old phone on his desk records the room (41 clips, audio). Nobody knows.
2. **Where the phone came from (fills world §8-4):** it was **his father's phone**. Pa drove a moto-taxi and used the
   phone's voice recorder to keep customers' directions. When the SIM lapsed after his death, Visal kept the phone.
   **The oldest file on the nearly full card is a voice memo of his father.** That is how he knew, before anyone, that
   "a voice stays." Storage pressure (D10) means that one day keeping a new clip may cost Pa's voice: a ready cost beat
   for series-plotter.
3. **His own clip (season-end well, world §8-5), proposal for twist-master:** before the job became his punishment, in
   Grade 10 semester 1, **Sophy** was the circle's target and Visal was one of the classmates who stayed silent (on
   record, the phone was already on his desk). He has never counted that file. Twist-master decides content within the
   rating rules.

### 2-5. Relationships
- Rachana: tormentor; the job began after he **refused to let her copy his answers** in front of the class (a small
  loss of face for her). The job is her mirror punishment; his revenge is his mirror answer.
- Dara: holds the phone he has to use; Visal sees he is afraid of the Coach and of his own father.
- Theara: the voice of most insults ("the Brick"); Visal knows Theara's laugh is too loud.
- Kolab: on clip 17 she whispers "Enough." (audible). He knows; she does not know he knows.
- Sophy: guilt and quiet recognition; the light bond (D10), never romance in Arc 1.
- Mealea: the only teacher who looks at him twice; he does not trust her yet.
- Yuthea: made him sign the apology letter "as if both were at fault".
- Mother Rany: love and silence; her rule is "Don't make trouble with rich people." (កុំរករឿងជាមួយអ្នកមាន។)

### 2-6. Arc
- EP01: cameraman in public, archivist in private. Micro-win: corrects a lie with an exact time. Twist: "Forty-one clips.
  Five voices." Sting: asked to film the parents' event.
- Arc 1: from waiting to acting; plays his first clip to the Coach; learns the cost (a bystander's face also falls);
  refuses one clip; Day 40 at the AV table.
- Arc 2: from proof to voice; must accept "Rolling." exposes him too (T-H4); the season ends when he plays, or says, his
  own silence.

### 2-7. Speech style (Khmer: short, natural, bubble-friendly)
- **Fewest words in the cast.** 1-6 words per bubble. Statements, not questions. Ends with ។ Counts and times instead of
  threats: "Forty-one." "Day twelve." "11:52." (spoken, never written in images).
- To adults: polite and minimal (បាទ / ខ្ញុំ). To peers: avoids pronouns entirely, no slang, never swears.
- Repeats other people's words back to them, flat (a verbal "play it back"): R: "Nobody will believe you." V: "Nobody."
- Never says "revenge". Never threatens. His rare long line is saved for an episode's turn.
- Signature lines (KM proposal for dialogue-writer): "ថត។" (Rolling.) · "សែសិបមួយឃ្លីប។ ប្រាំសំឡេង។" (Forty-one clips.
  Five voices.) · "ខ្ញុំនៅទីនោះ។" (I was there.)

## 3. Ouk Rachana — the Ringleader

### 3-1. Basics
- 17, Grade 11, 11C, row 2 aisle seat by the front door. First on the honour roll every year. Only daughter of the Ouk
  building-materials family; father works in the capital most of the month (off-page); **mother Bopha runs the yard and
  holds the committee seat**.
- Owns the rule **"Nothing lives past the bell."** (world R3). Proposal (fifth-voice plant, see §16): she first heard
  "Finish before the bell" from an adult at the 11C door and made it her law.

### 3-2. Want / Need / Fear
- **Want:** to stay flawless: top rank, perfect daughter, and total control of 11C, with nothing ever reaching home (T-S3).
- **Need:** to be someone, not her mother's trophy; to take responsibility instead of arranging it away.
- **Fear:** **her mother's disappointed look** (a small tightening of the mouth, never a shout). Losing first rank. Any
  school trouble reaching home in public.

### 3-3. Flaw
- Control and contempt as armour. She uses people as props and believes whatever she watches deleted is gone. Her caution
  (the supervised wipe) is exactly what built the archive.

### 3-4. Secrets
1. Last semester she slipped to second in a monthly exam; **Chanthy re-marked her paper upward** after the Ouk family paid
   for the extra-class fan and lights. Bopha does not know.
2. She chose Visal for the job because he refused her in public; it was never about "jokes". She has never told the
   group why.
3. She is afraid of the midday room too: she never sits during the job, always stands by the door.

### 3-5. Relationships
- Bopha (mother): worship and fear. Rachana switches register the moment her mother appears.
- Kolab: best friend since primary school; also leverage (Kolab's family rents its shop from the Ouk yard).
- Dara: useful muscle; his father drives an Ouk truck.
- Theara: entertainment; she lets him talk so she never has to.
- Yuthea and Chanthy: adults who smile at her family.
- Visal: the one person who said no to her.

### 3-6. Arc
- Arc 1 target, highest fall: the cold-open face at Day 40, her own voice playing to her mother and the hall.
- Consequence (proportional, D2/§7.8): loses honour-roll place, forced public apology, committee seat questioned.
  Aftertaste: Kolab's face also falls in the same clip.
- Arc 2 option for series-plotter: an unwilling witness against the adult who taught her the bell rule.

### 3-7. Speech style
- **Two registers, the cleanest in the cast.** To adults: soft, perfect polite Khmer (ចាស, លោកគ្រូ, អ្នកគ្រូ), full
  sentences, a smile in every line. To the circle: short commands and verdicts, "we" for inclusion (យើង), the softener
  ណា៎ used like a knife.
- Never raises her voice. Insults are cool and short: she calls Visal អ្នកថត, never his name.
- Signature lines: "Nothing lives past the bell." · "Delete it. I'm watching." · "Nobody believes a nobody."
