## 1. Series logline and season promise

- **Logline:** Forced to be his bullies' cameraman, Keo Visal kept every "deleted" clip on his dead father's mocked
  old phone; he plays each tormentor's own words to the one audience they fear, until the recordings point at the
  Deputy Principal who let it happen, and at a silence of his own he never counted.
- **Season 1 promise (24 episodes):** four students fall in Arc 1, the adult who permitted it falls in Arc 2, and the
  season ends when the Hero plays the one clip that convicts himself.
- **Engine check (concept §4):** every episode either spends a clip on the right audience (win) or threatens the
  archive, the Hero's access, or an ally (threat). Every played clip carries **one new piece of information** that
  points at the next target. Engine gears are placed: count (§6), fifth voice (Arc 2), hunt (EP08-10, EP14-18), cost
  (one cost beat per episode, one refusal per arc).
