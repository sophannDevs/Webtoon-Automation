## 0. Episode header

- **Goal:** the win of EP01 is paid for. The archive has a hard limit: the card is **full**, so the job goes on and
  nothing is kept. The school, through the bullies' own nickname, hands Visal a camera again. A father's voice, one
  line, in an earphone. Then the one person who was never supposed to matter says the sentence that ends the episode.
- **One-paragraph summary:** Day 2, 05:50: the dawn room, one socket, Visal counts "Thirty-nine days." and Rany unplugs
  her rice cooker so he can charge Pa's old phone. At 11:05 the job runs again; Theara jokes "Charge the Brick,
  Cameraman!", Visal's left thumb touches the side of the Brick (a tiny flat tone nobody hears under the laughter),
  Kolab is the only one who does not laugh and her eyes follow his hand; Rachana, between takes, asks what time it is
  ("Eleven-oh-nine.") and checks her pink phone. Night: the Brick refuses again, twice. "Charged." "Full." "Nothing
  from today." "One in. One out." "Forty-one. Not one." Day 3 on the court: Coach Tola announces trials in five days
  and says "A captain is clean, or he is nothing."; Dara answers with a stiff "Yes, Coach!"; the province wants the
  trials filmed, Theara shouts "We have a Cameraman!", the room laughs, and Tola hands Visal the school's camera.
  Another job at noon, again for nothing. Dusk: a moto-taxi engine idling at the rank; night: a four-second lesson
  tail, his thumb stops it, then Pa's one line in the earphone. Day 4: Rachana, who checked, says "Ten fifty-two.
  Nobody remembers a minute." Visal: "I count." At 16:35 the corridor is empty except for Kolab at the railing.
  "Visal." (the first time anyone in the circle uses his name). Rachana's voice from below. Kolab: "I saw your thumb.
  On the Brick."
