## 0. Episode header

- **Goal:** the first clip is spent. Visal turns down the crowd and the public exposure the reader expects and gives **one
  audience, alone**: Coach Tola, in a room with a door that closes. The Coach's own rule, said aloud again, convicts the boy he
  was about to make captain.
- **One-paragraph summary:** Day 5, the 07:00 line-up: Kolab, three places ahead, twists her bag strap and does not turn; Rachana
  whispers in her ear and then tells Visal only that Dara's phone is charged for noon (she knows nothing). At noon the job runs
  again, the Brick gives its tiny flat refusal tone (card still full), Kolab's eyes go to his hand and then to Rachana's back, and
  say nothing. A small Grade 10 teammate, Pich, taps on the door glass with white tape on his fingers copying Dara's; Dara's face
  turns warm for him and goes cold again when the door shuts. Day 6, the court: Coach Tola, "Trials. Two days.", names the
  captain after the last match and tells Dara "You're first on my list. Don't give me a reason."; Dara answers with the stiff
  "Yes, Coach!"; the Coach puts Visal with the school camera "Here. Where I stand." and says the camera goes back to the equipment
  room after every session. In the corridor Kolab stops, opens her mouth, and walks on. Day 7, night: Visal listens to the head
  of one clip in the earbud ("Twenty-three."), stops it before its last line, and decides: "Playing costs nothing." / "Not the
  crowd. One." Day 8, the trials: Dara spikes, Pich cheers, Theara and Rachana jeer at the Cameraman, Kolab stands and walks along
  the bleachers straight toward the net (the reader is sure she will tell) and goes past them to the water jug. Visal asks the
  Coach "Two minutes. About the captain." and is given the equipment room after the last match. Dara wins the final point and, on
  his way past, says the live "Hurry up, Cameraman." In the equipment room, door shut, Visal plays the head of clip 23 to the
  Coach alone: "Door's covered." / "Hurry up, Cameraman." / his own "Rolling." / "Relax. Even upstairs just walks past." The
  Coach is silent a long time, then says his own rule back: "A captain is clean, or he is nothing." "And you held the camera."
  "Yes." Visal leaves. The Coach crosses the court to the celebrating squad and says flat: "Sum Dara. My office." Visal, far off,
  whispers "Forty." The last panel is Dara's face falling.
