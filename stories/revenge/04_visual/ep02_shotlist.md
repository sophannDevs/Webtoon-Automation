## Global rules for every panel (prompt-smith: carry into each prompt)

1. **The abusive act is never shown through a lens.** No panel shows what Dara's black phone or the school camera films, no
   target of the "joke", no injury, no touch, no screen content, no recording interface. Job panels (P07-P18, P43) show only
   hands, faces, stripes of light, the half-closed door. Dara's black phone is seen **edge-on, from the side, or screen-side
   only (plain dark)**, never with its rear lens toward the camera. From behind Visal (P14) the phone's screen faces us.
2. **Text-free.** Blackboard clean dark green, clipboard blank page, school camera body and strap plain, wall clocks absent or
   without numerals, phone screens dark or plain faint glow, tees and PE kit plain (no prints, no numbers), motorbike plates plain
   dark, rice cooker plain, doors without numbers. The Brick's orange sticker has no print.
3. **Colour guard.** Olive #6B7A3A = the Brick only (pocket edge, back, desk corner); the school camera is **charcoal** (never
   olive, never khaki); pearl-pink = Rachana's clip and phone only; Rany's blouse is **blue check**; Kolab's scrunchie is marigold.
   Motorbikes at the rank: black, red, blue, grey, white. Foliage deep blue-green.
4. **The Brick.** Same spot in every desk panel: window-side, board-side corner (his top-left as he sits), **face-down, lens up,
   one clean strip of tape**. Face-up only in the night/dawn room panels (P01, P19-P23, P46-P48). In his pocket: only the top
   ~2 cm of olive edge, **left shirt pocket** (viewer's right when he faces us). The school camera strap goes over his **right**
   shoulder so nothing covers the left pocket.
5. **Light grammar:** job = hard noon stripes (L3) only at S2 and S5; court = L4 glare with shade under the awning; dusk rank =
   L6 amber fading to blue (no style-bible code; art-director may name it L6b); room = L7 (one bare bulb); playback = L9 cool
   blue-grey cast on the listener's face; Day 4 classroom = L2 with open louvres, flat glare at the windows (NOT the barred
   stripes); corridor 16:35 = L6.
6. **Screen direction:** 11C master view = from the teacher's desk to the back, window side = screen right, door side = screen
   left. Reverse views flip it and are marked `REVERSE`. Corridor: **railing always on the yard side**; stair end = screen left,
   11C door end = screen right in P54-P60; the stair itself and 11C's door never share a frame (style-bible §9 rule 16).
7. **Key rhymes the art must hold:** P06 with EP01 P05 (same top-down insert, one clean strip) · P11, P21, P23, P43 (the thumb at
   the side of the Brick, same crop and size) · P24 with EP01 P25/P54 (the Brick on a flat surface) · **P40 and P60** (Kolab at
   the same frame position left of centre, looking screen-right; P40 olive edge soft in the background, P60 olive edge soft in
   the foreground) · P42 (grey camera and olive pocket edge in one frame: two cameras) · P01 and P19 (same room, same layout) ·
   P46 and P48 (same framing family, cool cast) · P45 and P48 (same engine-idle sound zone).
8. **Heights (two-shots):** Dara 182 > Tola 175 > Visal 168 > Theara 163 > Rachana 160 > Kolab 155. Rany about 150.
9. Bubble kinds in `dialogue_ref`: normal · whisper · PLAYBACK-EARBUD (tail to the earbud wire) · ECHO (memory edge, dashed,
   no device tail) · off-panel (faint, tail toward the edge) · sfx · caption.
10. Rating 15+: nobody is touched on screen; laughter is shown by mouths and shoulders only.
