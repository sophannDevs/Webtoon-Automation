## Global rules for every panel (prompt-smith: carry into each prompt)

1. **The abusive act is never shown through the lens.** No panel shows what Dara's black phone films, no target of the
   "joke", no injury, no touch, no screen content, no recording interface. The job panels show only hands, faces, the
   stripes of light and the shut louvres. Camera never sits square on a phone's back camera: Dara's phone is seen from the
   side, edge-on, or screen-side only (plain dark or plain glow).
2. **Text-free.** Blackboard clean dark green, no chalk words. Notice boards, banners, ribbons, garlands, folders, exercise
   books, badges, cloth pouch, tiffin and trophies carry no marks. Wall clock (Staff Office) has plain ticks, no numerals.
   Doors have no numbers. Phone screens are dark or plain glow. The Brick's orange sticker has no print.
3. **Phones.** The Brick: olive-green case worn shiny at the edges, diagonal crack across the bottom-right corner held by
   yellowed clear tape, small round orange blank sticker near the single round lens, spiderweb crack on the screen's
   bottom-right corner. **Day 1 = one clean strip of tape. Day 40 (P04 only) = two strips.** Dara's phone: large matte
   black. Rachana's: pearl-pink case, pale-pink hair clip.
4. **Light grammar (world §9):** job = hard noon stripes through shut louvres; Deputy room = cool, flat, air-con, closed
   blinds; 13:40 = flat white-hot glare outside, louvres open; 16:30 = long amber; shed = deep shade against bright amber
   yard; Day 40 hall = even bright public light; playback = cool desaturated blue-grey cast on listeners' faces.
5. **Key rhymes the art must hold:** P25 and P55 (same Brick scale, same frame position, same tilt; face-down vs face-up)
   · P01 and P60 (New Hall at the same frame position and size; Day 40 full vs Day 1 closed) · P07 and P35 (same hands
   angle, tenser) · P40 background and P56 inset (the Brick in the noon stripe) · P19 and P48 and P52 (the Brick's olive
   top edge in Visal's LEFT shirt pocket, same height) · P03 is a reusable layout for EP11.
6. Bubble kinds in `dialogue_ref`: normal · whisper · PLAYBACK-SPEAKER · PLAYBACK-DEVICE · ECHO · sfx · caption.
