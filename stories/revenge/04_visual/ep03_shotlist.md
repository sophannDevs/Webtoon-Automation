## Global rules for every panel (prompt-smith: carry into each prompt)

1. **The abusive act is never shown, and nothing is ever shown through a lens.** No panel shows what Dara's black phone or the
   school camera films, no target of the "joke", no injury, no touch, no screen content, no recording interface.
   - Job panels (P07-P15) show only hands, faces, stripes of light, the half-closed door. **Dara's black phone is seen edge-on or
     screen-side only (plain dark), never with its rear lens toward the camera.** At P13 the script says "back toward us": it is
     drawn from behind and above Visal's right shoulder so the phone's **back faces the room (away from us)** and its plain dark
     screen faces us. See open points.
   - **The school camera** (P16, P23, P24, P34, P41, P47, P59-P60): charcoal body, **flip-screen dark and blank, turned toward the
     operator or toward us, never showing a picture**; the lens end always points away from the viewer. The view it takes is never shown.
   - **Clip 23 playback (P28-P29 earbud, P49-P52 device):** drawn only as **faces plus a plain dark phone**. The Brick is shown
     **screen-up (dark, spiderweb-cracked screen, olive edges) or edge-on**; its back with the lens never faces the camera in a
     playback panel. No sound waves, no icons, no screen content. The clip's "joke" is never played, described or shown: at night the
     sound stops right after "Rolling." (P30); in the equipment room it stops right after the cover line (P52).
2. **Text-free.** Blackboard clean dark green; clipboards blank (edge-on or blank page); the school camera body, strap and case plain
   (no logo, no label); wall clock absent or without numerals; phone screens dark or plain faint glow; tees, PE kit and towel plain
   (no prints, no numbers); scoreboard flip-cards, ball-bag tags and shelf tags blank; water jug and cups plain; doors without
   numbers, door plates or signs; the Brick's orange sticker has no print; no calendar in the hero's room.
3. **Colour guard.** Olive #6B7A3A = the Brick only (pocket edge, back, desk corner, the palm in P48). The school camera is
   **charcoal**. Pearl-pink = Rachana's clip and phone case only (in a bleacher shot those are the only pink pixels). Rany's blouse
   is blue check. Kolab's scrunchie is marigold (left wrist). Foliage deep blue-green.

     EP03 additions). He is never in teal and never in navy.
