## Summary

Top 5 hottest sub-patterns for 2026 school revenge (with one-line reason):

1. **"Teach-a-lesson" justice (참교육 서사)** — at peak. Netflix *Teach You a Lesson* (참교육, from Naver
   webtoon *Get Schooled*) released 2026-06-05, hit #1 global non-English and pulled the source webtoon
   and its studio YLAB back into the spotlight. Why now: public fatigue that "the system does not fix
   bullying / collapsed teacher authority" (교권 붕괴); readers want someone to make it fair. [Observed]
2. **Masked / hidden-identity avenger (정체 숨긴 응징자)** — rising-to-peak. *Psycho Revenge* (싸이코 리벤지,
   Naver): a brother whose sibling died because of bullies becomes the masked streamer "Neon Mask" and
   punishes bully gangs. Why now: double life gives a reveal engine every episode; streaming = public
   exposure. [Observed, status uncertain — see below]
3. **Outsider enters the school to avenge someone (복수를 위해 학교로 들어온 외부인)** — rising. *Sachae
   Jigan* (사채지간, loan shark enters a school to dig up the truth of his sister's death; review 2026-09),
   *Returning Student*, *My Strange Hero*. Why now: an adult competence fantasy inside a powerless space.
   [Observed]
4. **Regression revenge in school (회귀 학원 복수)** — steady, strong in female-lead lane. *The Price Is
   Your Everything* (Naver, from 2023-10; reported 43.9M reads in a 2026 list): a lifelong bullying victim
   goes back in time and re-enters the academy to repay those who ruined her. Why now: "I know what you
   will do" = total control fantasy (통제감). [Observed; read count is secondary-source]
5. **Revenge-by-proxy / investigation thriller (복수 대행 · 추리형 복수)** — steady (drama-led). *Revenge of
   Others* (3인칭 복수) and the post-*The Glory* (더 글로리) lineage: revenge as a planned, evidence-driven
   case. Why now: smarter, slower-burn payoff that avoids pure fist-fighting. [Observed lineage; current
   heat Inferred]

Biggest single signal of 2026: the 참교육 hit **also** produced loud criticism that it "justifies violence
as punishment" and is "cathartic but empty" (통쾌하지만 공허하다). That is a gap our brief (no bodily
violence as payoff; evidence, timing, exposure) can step into. [Observed + Inferred]

## Genre Trends (sub-patterns of 학원 복수물)

### 1. Teach-a-lesson justice (참교육 / 응징 서사)
- **Core tropes:** a stronger figure arrives and punishes bullies with "an eye for an eye" (눈에는 눈);
  adults who enable bullying (parents, corrupt teachers) are punished too.
- **Variations:** state-backed enforcer (참교육's 교권보호국 supervisor); punishment extended from students to
  abusive parents and educators (Korea Times, 2026-08-20 headline: "not just unruly students").
- **Lifecycle:** **Peak** (2026-06 to 2026-08). Signals: global #1 non-English, 88-country Top 10 within
  4 days (FlixPatrol via MT, 2026-06-10); H1 2026 most-watched list #6 TV (search snippet, Esquire/
  Harper's area); political debate and a real "교권보호단" follow-on (MTN 2026-07-15). [Observed]
- **Counter-signal (keep both sides):** critics say it glorifies vigilante violence / corporal punishment
  (Tatler Asia, Herald, Wi Geun-u quoted in coverage); the source webtoon had racism/misogyny
  controversies and was pulled from Naver's North American service in 2023; teachers called it
  "통쾌하지만 공허" (Daum desk column 2026-06-25); a lawyer column (Lawtimes) says extra-legal punishment
  "leaves a bitter aftertaste". Audience side: RT audience 92% reported mid-June. [Observed]
- **Reader desire:** fairness (공정함) and relief of real-world helplessness — MTN (2026-06-26) says the hit
  is not explained by 사이다 alone, but by social fatigue over teacher-rights collapse plus Netflix reach.
- **Source:** MT 2026-06-10, MTN 2026-06-26 / 2026-07-15 / 2026-08-25, Vogue KR 2026-06-08, Lawtimes,
  Daum column 2026-06-25, Tatler Asia, Korea Times 2026-08-20.

### 2. Masked / hidden-identity avenger (정체 숨긴 응징자 · 이중생활)
- **Core tropes:** victim-side person (sibling, friend) builds a second identity; punishes publicly
  (streamer, anonymous account); the school does not know who it is.
- **Example:** *Psycho Revenge* (싸이코 리벤지, Naver, Monday) — Cha Seong-bin, after his only family (younger
  sibling) dies due to high-school bullies, becomes "Neon Mask" and takes down bully gangs (Insight, dated
  2026-10-16 in search; says serialized since "Sept 6 last year").
- **[Uncertain] conflict:** MangaUpdates lists the series as "73 chapters + prologue (Canceled)". Also the
  Insight article ID is low for a 2026 article and another Insight item in results showed a date/content
  mismatch, so the "2026-10" date may be a re-stamp. **Do not treat as a confirmed fresh hit.** Ask
  platform-ranker to check Naver status.
- **Lifecycle:** **Rising-to-peak** as a mechanism (hidden identity + public exposure). [Inferred]
- **Reader desire:** control and anonymity — the weak side holds the information advantage.
- **Note for us:** the masked-fighter version is violence-led. The hidden-identity *mechanism* transfers
  cleanly to a non-violent, evidence-led story.

### 3. Outsider enters the school (외부인 잠입 복수)
- **Core tropes:** an adult or older person poses as / becomes a student to avenge a dead or broken
  victim (sister, orphanage friend); conflict between revenge and loyalty.
- **Examples:** *Sachae Jigan* (사채지간; webtoonguide review 2026-09), *Returning Student* (Naver; friend
  bullied to death), *My Strange Hero* (falsely expelled man returns to the same school), *A Gangster
  Became a High Schooler* (possession variant).
- **Lifecycle:** **Rising** (2026 review activity), though the possession/gangster variant is crowded.
- **Reader desire:** competence fantasy — an adult's skills inside a space where kids are powerless.
- **Fit for our brief:** weak — our protagonist IS the bullied student. Use only as a supporting-character
  idea (e.g., an ally), not the core. [Inferred]

### 4. Regression / second chance (회귀 학원 복수)
- **Core tropes:** victim dies or hits bottom, wakes up years earlier in school, uses future knowledge
  to dismantle bullies and fake friends; often a romance or ally subplot.
- **Examples:** *The Price Is Your Everything* (Naver, story Gones / art Haco, from 2023-10-18; EN since
  2024-02-27; female lead, "calculating" revenge, betrayal by trusted friends), *Devil Returns to School
  Days* (Naver, 2023-04 to 2025-03; time-travel tag, rooftop fall, mechanics unconfirmed).
- **Lifecycle:** **Peak / plateau** — regression (회빙환) is the most saturated device in the whole web-
  novel/webtoon market; inside school revenge it still works when the twist is in *what* the hero knows,
  not the time jump itself. [Inferred]
- **Reader desire:** control (통제감) — "I already know your next move".
- **Risk:** regression removes suspense if the hero always wins; needs a "future changes" complication.

### 5. Investigation / proxy revenge (추리형 · 대행 복수)
- **Core tropes:** revenge as a planned case: collect proof, map the bully network, strike at the moment
  of maximum public exposure; often a whodunit (who really caused the death).
- **Lineage:** *The Glory* (2022-12/2023-03, long-term planned revenge) → *Revenge of Others* (3인칭 복수,
  revenge-for-hire high-teen thriller) / *Weak Hero* / *The King of Pigs* → 참교육 (Daum feature on why
  school violence became an OTT staple; Harper's/Elle coverage).
- **Lifecycle:** **Steady** in drama; fewer clear webtoon examples found in 2026 searches (data gap).
- **Reader desire:** fairness + intellect — the payoff is earned by planning, not by power.

### 6. School action / fighting (학원 액션 · 일진물) — context
- Still a large lane: *Lookism*, *Study Group* (S2 in YLAB lineup, 2026-07-28), *Viral Hit*, *Reality
  Quest* (bullied student levels up via game system), *To Not Die*, *Hectopascal*, *King Game* (ended
  2026-03, 129 eps), *Designated Bully* (S3 ended 2026-05). [Observed, regional listicle 2026-09]
- Long-standing critique: "school as battlefield, students as fighters / 일진", action pushes everything
  else aside; 일진 glorification debates (Khan weekly 2022; webtoonguide column 2017). Past context only.

## Genre Hybrids

1. **Regression × evidence revenge** (회귀 × 증거 복수): future knowledge tells the hero *where proof will
   exist* (which phone, which chat, which day). Keeps control fantasy, swaps fists for proof. [Inferred;
   strong fit]
2. **Hidden identity × exposure** (정체 은닉 × 폭로): the bullied kid runs an anonymous channel/account (or
   is secretly the person everyone fears/admires) and releases truth in stages. Signal: *Psycho Revenge*,
   *Revenge of Others*. [Inferred; strong fit]
3. **Revenge × mystery** (복수 × 추리): "who really caused it" — the obvious bully is a pawn; the real
   mastermind is a friend/teacher/parent. Signal: *The Price Is Your Everything* (betrayal by trusted
   people), *Returning Student*. [Observed pattern]
4. **Revenge × protector pact** (복수 × 계약 동맹): victim + ally with a secret cost. Signal: *Deathwish
   Covenant* (2026-04). [Observed, thin]
5. **Revenge × system/game** (복수 × 시스템): *Reality Quest*, *Viral Hit* — mostly power-up/fight lane;
   **poor fit** for our no-violence rule. [Observed]

## Planning Implications

### What makes the payoff feel satisfying (사이다의 조건)
1. **Mirror justice (되돌려주기):** the bully receives a consequence shaped like their own act (they
   filmed/humiliated → they are exposed on the same stage). 참교육 coverage names "눈에는 눈" as the core
   catharsis. Translate it to *mirrored exposure*, not mirrored violence. [Observed → adapted]
2. **Earned by preparation:** show the setup (clue, recording, timing) before the strike; the reader
   should be able to re-read and find the plant. Fair twist = clue planted earlier.
3. **Public moment + witnesses:** the payoff lands where the bully's status lives (class, assembly,
   group chat, parents' meeting). Status loss in front of peers is the 15+-safe equivalent of a knockout.
4. **Hit the enablers too:** 2026 audiences want adults/systems held accountable (Korea Times 2026-08-20).
5. **Short frustration, fast relief (고구마 → 사이다):** keep humiliation scenes brief and shown through
   faces/silence; cash at least one small 사이다 per episode, a big one every arc. [Inferred from 사이다
   culture; audience-analyst to confirm]
6. **Aftertaste is a feature:** the strongest critique of 2026's hit was "empty". A beat where the hero
   chooses *not* to become the bully (or pays a cost) gives weight without slowing the hit. [Inferred]

### Recommended (for the 3 fresh-twist options in Phase 2)
- **A. Evidence regression:** hero returns to the day before the worst incident, knowing exactly where
  the proof is and who will betray him. Twist engine: the future changes, and a "friend" is the
  architect.
- **B. Anonymous exposer:** the class's feared anonymous account that leaks secrets is secretly the
  bullied student; each episode releases one piece of proof; the bullies hunt the account inside the
  class.
- **C. Realistic justice case:** hero builds a lawful case (own recordings, diary, witnesses, 학폭위-style
  hearing) while bullies and a powerful parent try to bury it; twist each episode from a witness flipping
  or a hidden accomplice.
- All three avoid bodily violence as payoff and fit 15+.

### Cautions
- Do not make revenge crueler than the bullying (reads as "empty" / new bully).
- Third-party secret recording is legally grey in Korea; if used, let characters debate it or use the
  victim's own recordings. Avoid presenting doxxing of real-looking personal data as heroic.
- Cambodian readers: no data found on Cambodian webtoon/revenge preference; Korean Wave strong since late
  2000s and Khmer-translated Korean romance novels popular (Phnom Penh Post). A light romance/ally
  thread may help. **[Uncertain — no direct data]**
- Avoid real-person or real-school references (the real 2026 학폭 controversies in sports/entertainment are
  context only).
