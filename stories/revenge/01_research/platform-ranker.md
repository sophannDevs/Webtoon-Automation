### 1.2 Common structural patterns (cross-title)

1. **School revenge is a Naver-dominant shelf.** Every confirmed school-bullying revenge hit found is on Naver/WEBTOON
   (Monday slot recurs: 참교육, 싸이코 리벤지). KakaoPage revenge hits skew romance-fantasy / adult revenge. [V]/[E]
2. **Drama adaptations keep re-igniting the shelf** (Weak Hero 2025, Get Schooled 2026, Kim Bu-jang 2026, Viral Hit
   announced 2026). "Bully gets exposed / re-educated" is mainstream in 2026, not niche. [V]
3. **Most top school-revenge titles pay off with fists.** Physical action is the default payoff (Viral Hit, Weak
   Hero, Psycho Revenge). Our brief forbids bodily violence as the main payoff, so **evidence / exposure / timing
   revenge is a less crowded lane** (closer to 참교육's "system punishes bullies" fantasy and The Glory-style
   planned revenge). [E]
4. **K-사이다 formula:** punishment starts almost at the same time as the wrong, before the reader gets frustrated
   (column on 모범택시 / 더 글로리 / 범죄도시 logic, DDaily). [S]
5. **Violent revenge carries a stability risk.** Psycho Revenge has conflicting status (ongoing vs. canceled at ep
   73). Whatever the cause, it shows that ultra-violent bully revenge is volatile. [V conflict; cause unknown]

## 3. Serial structure (연재 구조 해부)

### 3.1 Episode length

| Metric | Value | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Panels (컷) per episode, Korean weekly serial | **60–90 cuts**, all color, is the long-cited workload; platforms historically asked for 70–90 cuts per episode (Webtoonguide) | [S] (unverified as a 2026 average) |
| Practical cut count of action/school hits | about 50–80 cuts per episode; fight or reveal episodes run longer | [E] |
| Scroll reading time | about 3–6 minutes per episode | [E] |
| Our harness floor | **50+ panels** = within the industry band (lower-middle) | fits |

### 3.2 Pacing (회차 호흡)

- **Weekly, one episode per week.** Naver adds paid 미리보기 (preview): the next 1 to 3+ episodes are sold ahead with
  cookies. Naver introduced the preview model in 2012 and cookies in 2016. [V]
- **Episode = one beat unit.** In revenge titles, a typical episode is: humiliation or pressure, then a counter-move
  setup, then a partial payoff or a reveal, then the hook. [E]
- **Arc unit:** school-revenge series run in arcs of about 8–15 episodes, one target (bully) per arc, building up
  the ladder: lackey, then the ringleader, then the protector (parent, teacher, school board). [E, standard for
  참교육 / 약한영웅 style arcs]

### 3.3 First big payoff (첫 사이다)

| Payoff | Typical position in hits | Label |
|---|---|---|
| Micro-payoff (protagonist says or does something that shifts power) | **within EP01**, often in the last third | [E] |
| First clear punishment of a minor bully | **EP02–EP04** | [E] |
| First big public payoff (exposure, the ringleader humiliated) | **end of the first arc, about EP08–EP15** | [E] |
| Rule from the K-사이다 formula | punishment should begin "before the reader feels frustrated" (DDaily column) | [S] |

Reasoning: readers drop revenge titles when the 고구마 (frustration, "sweet potato") phase runs without relief. Hits
put the humiliation in the first half of EP01 and show the protagonist's capacity or a plan by the end of EP01.
No 2026 quantitative drop data was found. **[?]**

## 4. Cliffhanger map (클리프행어 맵)

| Pattern | Where | How it works in revenge / school titles | Strength |
|---|---|---|---|
| Evidence revealed (증거 공개 직전) | last 2–3 panels | phone, recording or file shown; content hidden until next episode | strong |
| Target realizes (가해자의 깨달음) | last panel | close-up of the bully's face as he understands who is behind it | strong |
| Counter-ambush (역습 / 판 뒤집기) | last 3–5 panels | protagonist's plan is exposed or reversed; a new threat appears | strong (good before the paywall) |
| Declaration line (결정적 대사) | last panel | quiet line: "Now it's my turn." / "I recorded everything." | medium–strong |
| New ally / new enemy arrives (충격 등장) | last panel | a transfer student, parent or teacher enters | medium |
| Quiet aftermath (여운) | end of arc | the payoff lands; one unresolved thread is teased | weak by design (used right after big payoffs) |

All [E], based on genre convention and the observation-frames checklist. Not measured per title this run.

- **Placement rule:** the last 2–3 panels are a build-up (silence, close-up, single line); the final panel hides the
  key information. In a vertical scroll, a long blank gap before the last panel raises tension. [E]
- **Revenge-specific:** alternate "win" endings (사이다 cliff: the bully's face falls) and "threat" endings
  (고구마 cliff: the protagonist is cornered). Two threat endings in a row is the classic drop-risk zone. [E]

### 5.2 Where readers open their wallet (analysis)

- **Conversion happens at a cliffhanger, not at a payoff.** Readers pay to resolve tension; a payoff episode before
  the paid line lowers the urge to pay. [E]
- **Kakao-style binge breakpoint:** titles commonly launch with a free opening block (often stated as about 3–10
  episodes, varying by title and campaign: **[?]**). The episode right at the free/paid boundary should carry the
  **strongest cliffhanger of the opening run**, ideally the moment just before the first big payoff
  ("the evidence is about to drop"). [E]
- **Naver weekly-preview pressure:** every free episode's ending is a sales pitch for 1–3 preview episodes. So
  **every** episode needs a strong-type ending (evidence / realization / counter-ambush), not only the
  paywall one. [E]
- **Hook density in the paid run:** paid episodes should give at least one partial payoff each (a satisfaction
  receipt) plus a new cliffhanger. Reader complaints of "paid and nothing happened" are the classic payment
  complaint (결제 불만) pattern. [E, per the observation-frames coding table]

## 6. Planning implications for `revenge` (기획 시사점)

1. **Episode length:** keep **55–70 panels** per episode (above the 50+ floor, inside the 60–90 industry band). Use
   longer (70+) only for exposure / reveal episodes. [E based on S]
2. **EP01 structure (recommended):**
   - Panels 1–5: cold open on the end-state hook (e.g., the bully's face as something is exposed), then "3 weeks
     earlier". This plants the payoff promise.
   - Panels 6–25: humiliation shown through dialogue, faces and silence (15+). Keep it short; the 고구마 must not
     exceed about 40% of EP01.
   - Panels 26–45: the protagonist's edge is revealed (evidence, recording, a rule or system they have found):
     the micro-사이다.
   - Panels 46–end: the twist (one per episode) and a strong cliffhanger (evidence about to drop, or the target
     realizes).
3. **First big payoff:** first minor bully punished by **EP03–EP04**; the first ringleader-level public exposure at
   the end of arc 1 (**about EP08–EP12**). Do not delay past EP15. [E]
4. **Cliffhanger rotation:** alternate win-cliffs and threat-cliffs; never two threat-cliffs in a row; the last
   2–3 panels are a silent build-up with the key info hidden.
5. **Paywall-friendly design:** treat every episode end as a preview sales pitch (Naver logic). If a free/paid
   boundary is defined (Kakao logic), put it on the episode **just before** the first big exposure. Each paid
   episode gives at least one partial payoff.
6. **Differentiation lane:** the 2026 school-revenge shelf is dominated by physical fights (Viral Hit, Weak Hero,
   Psycho Revenge). **Evidence / exposure / timing revenge** (brief-compliant) is a less saturated lane that matches
   the 참교육 "system finally punishes" fantasy and the morally-weighted 고백 angle, with lower violence and
   stability risk. [E]
7. **Khmer-edition note:** the language change does not alter structure. Cambodian-reader data was not found; the
   closest proxy is the SEA (Indonesia) appetite for school / bullying and revenge manhwa. [S]
