# Panel prompts for Google Flow (EP01, 「장갑을 벗기 전에」)

- Written by prompt-smith, 2026-11-20. Flow-by-hand mode, Khmer edition. 62 panels, one block each.
- Built from: `00_input/brief.md` (locked decisions, rulings A–J), `04_visual/style-bible.md`, `04_visual/character-sheets.md`,
  `04_visual/ep01_shotlist.md`, `04_visual/ep01_lettering.md` (the empty areas), `04_visual/refs/ref_prompts.md` (the 10
  reference cards), `03_episode/ep01_beatsheet.md` §1 and `02_story/twist-plan.md` (EP1).

## How to use these prompts

1. Make the 10 reference cards first (`refs/ref_prompts.md`) and check them. Every panel leans on them.
2. Keep the same Flow image model for all cards and all 62 panels (a new model changes the drawing style).
3. For each panel: attach the images listed in `attach:` (if Flow accepts reference images), set the ratio from
   `aspect:`, paste the whole `prompt:` text and generate. Make 2 versions (4 for panels 1, 13, 18, 19, 40, 45, 46, 49,
   55 and 62) and keep the one that matches the reference cards best, not the prettiest one.
4. Save it with the exact file name in `output:`, or download into `_workspace/_inbox/` in panel order and say
   "import the inbox".
5. The pictures contain no text at all. The speech bubbles and the Khmer words are added later by the viewer, so every
   dialogue panel keeps one calm empty area, and paper, wax seals, signs and book spines are drawn blank.

## Order of work

- Reference cards first, then the panels scene by scene, each scene's wide shot first (panels 1, 6, 8, 13, 14, 21, 29,
  39 and 51 open their scenes).
- **Make panel_045 before panel_049.** Panel 49 attaches the finished `panel_045.png` and copies its camera exactly;
  only her face, her other four fingers, the thread and the fingertip mark change. If Flow can edit images, make panel
  49 by editing panel 45 with the panel 49 prompt.
- Optional, if Flow accepts one more image: for panels that echo an earlier one you may also attach that finished
  panel (panel 3 for 17, panel 5 for 47, panel 18 for 61, panel 36 for 37, panel 40 for 46), and panels 6 and 8 for the
  other lane and workshop panels (7, 9–12), because there is no workshop card.

## Aspect ratios

- 9:16 tall (7 panels): 1, 13, 19, 40, 46, 55, 62.
- 3:4 (17 panels): 2, 6, 12, 14, 16, 18, 21, 28, 29, 35, 43, 45, 47, 49, 51, 56, 58.
- 16:9 wide (3 panels): 4, 9, 37.
- 1:1 square: all the other 35 panels.
- If your Flow model has no 3:4, make those panels 1:1, except panel 2, which becomes 9:16. Panels 45 and 49 must
  always share the same ratio.

## Checking each result

- The 11 hardest panels (17, 18, 19, 34, 45, 46, 49, 54, 55, 56, 58) have a `note:` line saying what to check before you
  keep the image.
- Every image: no letters or numbers anywhere; her knotted thread on her LEFT wrist, his black glove on his LEFT hand,
  his loose lock of hair over his RIGHT brow. If one of these is on the wrong side the image is mirrored: generate it
  again, do not flip it. The empty bubble area must really be empty, and nobody may look young.
