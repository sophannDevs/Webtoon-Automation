**Make the cloud test first.** Make DANBI_wet_states alone and check it before anything else: it tests whether Flow can draw the series premise (a small rain cloud raining on her only, on a sunny day). If the rain spreads, the cloud sits on her head like a hat, or it looks like a speech bubble, it gets a `redraw_reason` and up to three redraws; if it still fails, tell the leader before making other cards. When it passes, make 02–06, then 07–14, then 15–16.

**How to check returned sheets.** Look at each sheet with these questions. One person: in every view the face, hair, outfit and height are the same, the turnaround matches the dry left figure of the cloud test, and each expression sheet matches its turnaround. Left/right marks: in a front view Taeo's brass watch (his left wrist) and Danbi's red strawberry clip (above her left ear) are both on the viewer's right, Taeo's pencil (his right ear) is on the viewer's left. One strong yellow: no yellow at all on the character sheets (01–03, 07, 08, 14; on 13 only the thread band and rib cap); on prop and place sheets yellow appears only on the yellow umbrella, the yellow thread, the rib cap or the one spool; the pencil is bare wood and all brass is dull brownish gold. No text anywhere: no labels, names, arrows, numbers, colour codes, watch numbers, logos, signatures or watermark, and signs, the shutter and the cart board carry no fake writing. Cloud (01, 13): one cloud, about a metre above her head, flat dark underside, no outline, a straight vertical column, dry ground outside it with the puddle only at her feet; it must never look like a balloon, halo or thought bubble. Props: grey tape lump on the bent rib, the yellow thread band readable at medium size, yellow rib cap, the two keys exactly the same shape, the hook a brass S-hook. Places: seen from the square the arch is on the left and the alley on the right, the hook sits at the centre of the shutter box, one keyhole, no people. Style: the same clean line art and soft cel shading as the rest, not photo-real, 3D or chibi, no panel border lines. If a sheet fails, note which check; ref-sheet-artist adds a `redraw_reason` to that block. After three redraws it is accepted with a flag.
